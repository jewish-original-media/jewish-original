## 1. Current scope and acceptance criteria

### Product bar

Jewish Original V1 is not a small published subset plus an unreviewed
backlog. Public launch of `jewishoriginal.com` requires a complete,
honest History archive and a finished Preview of the integrated site.

Positioning, unchanged:

> A modern home for Jewish history, culture, education, connection, and
> identity.

History is the foundation. Education transmits it. Identity is the
product. Connection is the outcome.

### MUST for public launch

| Criterion | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Complete unique History | Every unique, usable On This Day record across all 10 workbook tabs is edited, verified, deduplicated, and publicly accessible — or explicitly rejected / archived with a documented reason. |

**332 source rows**. The other eight workbook tabs are instructions,
unmaterialized pivots, calculated copies, or HTML projections. They add
reconciliation evidence, not extra articles.

The launch requirement is **every unique, usable story**, not a fixed
quota. **298** is the generated unique-story count in History’s
machine-readable ledger, not a target to hit if later splits or merges
change the unique set.

### Sequence

1. Close P0 blockers H1–H4, A1, and A2.
2. Freeze a known-good Integration commit that includes the finished
   design/discovery pass and reads the completed published History set.

   `milestone-1-sanity-history` as history.

   the `*.vercel.app` production host **before** DNS.
6. Verify Production env: `NEXT_PUBLIC_SITE_URL=https://jewishoriginal.com`,
   Sanity project / dataset / read token, `DRAFT_MODE_SECRET`. Confirm
   `JEWISH_TODAY_ALLOW_PREVIEWS` and `INGEST_WRITES_ENABLED` are unset.
7. Smoke `/`, `/today`, `/history` (search, a filter, a date, page 2 if
   the published set needs it), five or more History articles, `/podcasts`
   and one episode, `/originals` (sample notice or final essays), `/news`
   if gated on, `/about`, `/support` (mailto), `/privacy`, 404.
8. Confirm Preview robots remain disallow-all and Production robots allow
   `/` except `/admin/` and `/api/`.
9. Attach `jewishoriginal.com` and `www` to Production only. Apex or www
   as the single canonical host; the other redirects. Do not attach the
   domain to a Preview branch.

    `/sitemap.xml`.

    step 9 is green.
