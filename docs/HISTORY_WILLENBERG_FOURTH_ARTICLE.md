## Fact review

Death date, Treblinka imprisonment, and participation in the 2 August 1943
revolt are verified. The stored encyclopedic body is not an On This Day
notice and contained two serious errors: a false definition of
Sonderkommando, and confusion of the Warsaw Ghetto Uprising with the 1944
Warsaw Uprising.

The public notice omits Sonderkommando, graphic camp-process detail, a
Treblinka death toll, and “last survivor of Treblinka.” It uses Yad Vashem’s
wording: one of the last survivors of Treblinka.

**Body:**

On this day, Samuel Willenberg died in Israel at the age of 93. Born in Częstochowa, Poland, he was deported to Treblinka in 1942 and forced to work there. On August 2, 1943, he took part in the prisoner revolt and escaped.

He later settled in Israel, where he wrote and made sculpture about what he had seen. Yad Vashem remembered him as one of the last survivors of Treblinka.
