# Jewish Original Media — Product Foundation

## Mission

Jewish Original Media is a modern home for Jewish history, culture, education,
connection, and identity. It serves Jews across denominations, backgrounds,
geographies, and levels of observance while maintaining an explicit,
nonpartisan commitment to Israel and the Jewish people.

## Product principle

**History is the foundation.**  
**Education transmits it.**  
**Identity is the product.**  
**Connection is the outcome.**

The product is an editorial publication and daily Jewish utility that grows
into an interactive platform. It is not a generic blog, news portal, podcast
brochure, or social network.

## Primary audiences

- Jews seeking an approachable daily connection to history and identity
- Students, educators, families, and lifelong learners
- Existing social followers arriving primarily on mobile
- Researchers, journalists, rabbis, schools, museums, and partner institutions
- Donors and sponsors seeking mission-aligned impact

## Initial product pillars

1. **Jewish Today:** trusted daily calendar context and a path into original work
2. **History:** permanent, structured, source-aware pages for the existing archive
3. **Original stories:** high-quality editorial work with strong internal linking
4. **Podcasts:** durable episode pages, transcripts, and related content
5. **Discovery:** restrained curation of news, culture, and events
6. **Support:** donations, sponsorships, and dedications that respect editorial trust

## Product standards

- Never fabricate Jewish history, Torah, news, sponsors, or community activity.
- Clearly label original, licensed, linked, partner, public-domain, and generated media.
- Make source transparency and image rights part of the publishing workflow.
- Prioritize mobile performance, accessible semantics, and permanent URLs.
- Automate reliable repetition; keep judgment, meaning, and voice with editors.
- Add community features around proven content behavior, not before it.

## V1 success criteria

- Editors can publish structured history entries and podcast pages without Cursor.
- Search engines can reliably crawl canonical, fast, internally linked pages.
- The homepage is useful today, not merely promotional.
- The archive can be imported, reviewed, and corrected without data loss.
- A three-person team can operate the system with documented workflows.
- Monetization placements are modeled without compromising reader trust.

## Explicitly out of scope for V1

- A general social network, LMS, or community forum
- An AI chatbot
- A full newsroom or indiscriminate feed aggregation
- Native applications before web product-market fit
- Complex personalization or a paid membership product
