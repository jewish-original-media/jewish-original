# Originals

Status: foundation only. No public Originals route. No homepage module while
no published documents exist.

Originals are JOM-owned editorial writing. They are not `blogPost`,
`newsletterPost`, or a generic posts collection.

## Public behavior now

- Not in navigation
- Not on the homepage
- No `/originals` placeholder route

## Minimum future article fields

When Originals are implemented, reuse the existing article document direction
from `docs/CONTENT_MODEL.md`:

- title
- slug
- author person references
- excerpt
- body
- sources / citations
- featured media with rights
- topics
- related History or Podcast references
- SEO
- publication / workflow

Do not invent article content to fill the homepage.
